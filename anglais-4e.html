Je retiens

📏 Comparatives : short adjective + -er + than (taller than) · long adjective : more + adj + than
🏆 Superlatives : the + -est / the most + adjective
✅ Present perfect : have/has + past participle (have been, has seen) · for, since, already, never, ever
🔮 will = décision ou prédiction · going to = projet ou signe visible
