Je retiens

⏪ Past simple : was/were · les verbes réguliers prennent -ed (played) · les irréguliers changent (went, saw, did)
❗ must / mustn't = obligation / interdiction · can = la capacité ou la permission
▶️ be + -ing = en train de faire · be going to / present + -ing = projet futur
📍 in (mois, année) · on (jour) · at (heure) · between (deux choses)
🔗 but (mais) · so (donc) · because (parce que)

Ex 1 · Le passé simple
  1. Yesterday, we ___ in London. → were
  2. I ___ my homework last night. → did
  3. She ___ the film at the cinema. → watched
  4. They ___ to Ireland last summer. → went
Ex 2 · Must, mustn't et can
  1. You ___ run in the corridor. It's dangerous. → mustn't
  2. You ___ wear a helmet on your bike. It's the law. → must
  3. My little sister ___ read yet. → can't
  4. « Can you swim? » — « Yes, I ___. » → can
Ex 3 · Be + -ing et le futur
  1. Look! It ___ now. → is raining
  2. We ___ to the cinema tonight. → are going
  3. He ___ his dinner now. → is eating
  4. Where is Tom? — He ___ a shower. → is taking
Ex 4 · Prépositions, vocabulaire et connecteurs
  1. The school is ___ the post office and the library. → between
  2. My maths lesson is ___ Tuesday at 9 a.m. → on
  3. I like rugby, ___ I don't like football. → but
  4. Quel mot signifie « un cours » dans un emploi du temps ? → lesson
Ex 5 · Past simple : négations et questions
  1. I ___ go to school yesterday. I was ill. → didn't
  2. ___ you play football last Saturday? → Did
  3. She didn't ___ her bike yesterday. → ride
  4. « Were you at home last night? » — « No, I ___. » → wasn't
  5. Where ___ they go on holiday last year? → did
Ex 6 · Verbes irréguliers au prétérit
  1. Last week, I ___ a letter to my friend. (write) → wrote
  2. We ___ a lot of photos on holiday. (take) → took
  3. She ___ her keys yesterday. (lose) → lost
  4. They ___ pizza for dinner last night. (eat) → ate
  5. He ___ a new bike last month. (buy) → bought
Ex 7 · Some, any, much, many
  1. I'd like ___ water, please. → some
  2. There aren't ___ apples left. → any
  3. How ___ sugar do you want? → much
  4. How ___ brothers have you got? → many
  5. There is ___ cheese on the table. → some
Ex 8 · Les adverbes de fréquence
  1. I ___ go to school on Sundays. (jamais) → never
  2. She ___ drinks tea at breakfast. (toujours) → always
  3. We ___ play tennis on Saturdays. (souvent) → often
  4. He ___ watches cartoons. (parfois) → sometimes
  5. They ___ eat fish. (rarement) → rarely
Ex 9 · In, on, at : les prépositions de temps
  1. My birthday is ___ May. → in
  2. The film starts ___ 8 o'clock. → at
  3. We go swimming ___ Saturdays. → on
  4. It's very cold ___ winter. → in
  5. We open our presents ___ Christmas Day. → on
Ex 10 · Les pronoms compléments
  1. This is my friend Tom. I like ___. → him
  2. Where is Lucy? I can't see ___. → her
  3. We are here! Look at ___! → us
  4. I love these shoes. I want to buy ___. → them
  5. Can you help ___, please? I'm lost. → me
Ex 11 · Le cas possessif ('s)
  1. Le vélo de Tom = … → Tom's bike
  2. La chambre de mes parents = … → my parents' room
  3. Les jouets des enfants = … → the children's toys
  4. La voiture de ma sœur = … → my sister's car
  5. La maison de mes grands-parents = … → my grandparents' house
Ex 12 · Questions au présent simple (do / does)
  1. ___ you like chocolate? → Do
  2. ___ your sister play the piano? → Does
  3. Where ___ they live? → do
  4. What time ___ Tom get up? → does
  5. « Does she speak English? » — « Yes, she ___. » → does
Ex 13 · La nourriture et les repas
  1. Comment dit-on « le pain » en anglais ? → bread
  2. Comment dit-on « le fromage » en anglais ? → cheese
  3. Comment dit-on « les légumes » en anglais ? → vegetables
  4. Comment dit-on « le poulet » en anglais ? → chicken
  5. Comment dit-on « le petit-déjeuner » en anglais ? → breakfast
Ex 14 · Was ou were ?
  1. I ___ at the cinema yesterday. → was
  2. They ___ very happy last week. → were
  3. ___ you at school on Monday? → Were
  4. She ___ tired after the match yesterday. → was
  5. We ___ in Spain last summer. → were
Ex 15 · Les nombres ordinaux
  1. Comment dit-on « premier » en anglais ? → first
  2. « 3rd » se lit… → third
  3. « 12th » se lit… → twelfth
  4. « 20th » se lit… → twentieth
  5. Comment dit-on « le 2 mai » en anglais ? → May the second
Ex 16 · Les métiers
  1. Comment dit-on « un boulanger » en anglais ? → a baker
  2. Comment dit-on « une infirmière » en anglais ? → a nurse
  3. Comment dit-on « un pompier » en anglais ? → a firefighter
  4. Comment dit-on « un agriculteur » en anglais ? → a farmer
  5. Comment dit-on « un vétérinaire » en anglais ? → a vet
Ex 17 · Le temps qu'il fait
  1. Comment dit-on « Il pleut » en anglais ? → It's raining
  2. Comment dit-on « Il neige » en anglais ? → It's snowing
  3. Comment dit-on « Il fait chaud » en anglais ? → It's hot
  4. Comment dit-on « Il y a du vent » en anglais ? → It's windy
  5. Comment dit-on « Le temps est nuageux » en anglais ? → It's cloudy
