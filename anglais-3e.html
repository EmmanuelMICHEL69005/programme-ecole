🔄 Passive : be + past participle (was written, is spoken, will be built)
💭 Second conditional : If + past simple, would + verbe (If I were rich, I would travel)
🗣️ Reported speech : will → would · is → was · present → past (he said that he liked)
⏪ Past perfect : had + past participle, for an action before another past action
