Je retiens

🧍 be : I am · you are · he/she/it is · we/you/they are
🎒 have got : I/you/we/they have got · he/she/it has got
🏃 Present simple : à la 3e personne, on ajoute -s (he plays) · négation : doesn't / don't
👨‍👩‍👧 Possessifs : my · your · his · her · its · our · their
✅ can = je peux · can't = je ne peux pas (pas de -s à la 3e personne)

Corrigé des exercices

Pour les parents : toutes les questions de la page avec leur réponse, pour vérifier ou refaire une séance sur papier. Ouvrez un exercice pour voir son corrigé.

Ex 1 · Be et have got
  1. She ___ eleven years old. → is
  2. We ___ a big garden. → have got
  3. They ___ from Scotland. → are
  4. Tom ___ two cats. → has got
Ex 2 · Le présent simple
  1. My dad ___ coffee every morning. → drinks
  2. We ___ football on Saturdays. → play
  3. She ___ to school at eight. → goes
  4. Lucas ___ like spiders. → doesn't
Ex 3 · Possessifs et famille
  1. This is my brother. ___ name is Tom. → His
  2. Julie has a cat. ___ cat is black. → Her
  3. The Smiths live in London. ___ house is big. → Their
  4. Comment dit-on « mère » en anglais ? → mother
Ex 4 · Can, can't et les prépositions
  1. Birds ___ fly. → can
  2. I ___ swim, but I can ride a bike. → can't
  3. The bank is ___ the park. → next to
  4. We get up ___ seven o'clock. → at
Ex 5 · Vocabulaire et grammaire du quotidien
  1. Comment dit-on « mercredi » en anglais ? → Wednesday
  2. Quel est le pluriel de « child » ? → children
  3. I ___ my teeth every evening. → brush
  4. It's 7:30. It's half ___ seven. → past
  5. There ___ two books on the table. → are
Ex 6 · Les mots interrogatifs
  1. ___ is your name? → What
  2. ___ do you live? — In Paris. → Where
  3. ___ old are you? → How
  4. ___ is your birthday? — In May. → When
  5. ___ is this girl? — She's my cousin. → Who
Ex 7 · There is / There are
  1. ___ a cat in the garden. → There is
  2. ___ three books on the table. → There are
  3. Is there a park near your house? — Yes, ___. → there is
  4. How many pupils ___ in your class? → are there
  5. There ___ two windows in my bedroom. → are
Ex 8 · A ou an ?
  1. I've got ___ apple. → an
  2. She has got ___ cat. → a
  3. He eats ___ egg every morning. → an
  4. There is ___ elephant at the zoo. → an
  5. My dad has got ___ car. → a
Ex 9 · Les pluriels irréguliers
  1. One child, two ___. → children
  2. One mouse, three ___. → mice
  3. One foot, two ___. → feet
  4. One man, four ___. → men
  5. One tooth, many ___. → teeth
