Je retiens

🧍 be : I am · you are · he/she/it is · we/you/they are
🎒 have got : I/you/we/they have got · he/she/it has got
🏃 Present simple : à la 3e personne, on ajoute -s (he plays) · négation : doesn't / don't
👨‍👩‍👧 Possessifs : my · your · his · her · its · our · their
✅ can = je peux · can't = je ne peux pas (pas de -s à la 3e personne)
